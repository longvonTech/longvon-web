How are pilot quantity and production MOQ decided?

For standard OEM orders using the existing Ring1 or Ring1C platform, the minimum order quantity (MOQ) is 1,000 units. Scope the customization your project actually needs

  • Existing-platform OEM: discuss the selected hardware, branding and packaging scope.
  • Custom ODM: evaluate industrial design, hardware and firmware changes with engineering before making a commitment.
  • Customer-owned App and partner SDK/API: confirm platforms, documentation, permissions and the agreed integration scope; no public self-serve SDK or unrestricted BLE promise.
  • Algorithms and cloud services: confirm authorized outputs and device/App/cloud responsibilities; many metrics depend on cloud services, not an all-offline assumption.

Prepare a useful quotation brief

Include target market, intended product/model/use case, sample and pilot estimates, annual forecast, required functions and integrations, target timing, and contact details. Unknown items can be discussed; an estimate is not a purchasing commitment. NDA or detailed technical review may be appropriate before sharing design files.

NPI path: EVT → DVT → PVT → mass production

For custom smart ring programs—especially brands bringing industrial design and PCB packages—LONGVON discusses a staged engineering validation path rather than jumping straight to catalog OEM. Stage definition, tooling and fees are confirmed after technical review; this page does not publish fixed price lists.

  1. Intake & NDA. Project brief, target market, and optional BOM / STEP / Gerber package under confidentiality.
  2. DFM review. Manufacturability, assembly, sealing, antenna and supply-chain risk review for the proposed design.
  3. EVT. Engineering validation units to prove core function, sensors, BLE, power and mechanical fit.
  4. DVT. Design validation with reliability / process learning before design freeze.
  5. PVT → MP. Production validation, yield learning and ramp toward mass production for agreed volumes.

Bring-your-own design & DFM review

If BOM, 3D STEP and PCB Gerber files are ready, LONGVON can review manufacturability under NDA and identify design-for-manufacturing improvements before EVT. Custom enclosure targets (including titanium), capacitive touch / gesture, haptic feedback, BLE, magnetic charging and companion app integration are scoped per project.

  • Hardware and PCB / PCBA discussion for miniaturized ring form factors
  • Firmware, BLE connectivity and companion App / SDK integration (project-scoped)
  • Enclosure and finishing options discussed per project (including titanium targets when applicable)
  • Haptic, capacitive touch / gesture, LED indicator and magnetic charging features when in scope
  • Manufacturing, assembly and quality discussion through evaluation to volume
