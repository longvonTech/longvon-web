    - paragraph:
      - strong: "Sleep outputs:"
      - text: sleep/wake detection, sleep stages, sleep duration, sleep score, respiratory rate, SpO2 trends, and any apnea-risk screening.
    - paragraph:
      - strong: "Algorithm ownership:"
      - text: whether the sleep algorithm is licensed, white-labeled, or developed exclusively for you; request validation data and confusion matrices against polysomnography or a validated wearable reference.
    - paragraph:
      - strong: "App and data:"
      - text: native iOS/Android app, SDK/API, cloud hosting, data export, user consent flows, and whether you can integrate the data into your own platform.
    - paragraph:
      - strong: "Hardware:"
      - text: battery capacity and real-world battery life, charging case, waterproof rating, materials, sizing range, BLE version, and production-test yield.
    - paragraph:
      - strong: "Compliance:"
      - text: intended use determines the pathway—general wellness claims differ substantially from medical claims such as sleep-apnea detection or diagnosis. Do not market “sleep apnea detection” without appropriate regulatory and clinical evidence.
