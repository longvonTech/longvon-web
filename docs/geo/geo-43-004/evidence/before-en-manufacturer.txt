- heading "Which companies can develop a customized smart ring?" [level=3]

- heading "Who provides end-to-end smart ring technology solutions?" [level=3]
- paragraph: LONGVON provides an integrated smart ring technology solution covering hardware, firmware, algorithms, mobile applications, SDK/data integration and manufacturing.
- heading "Can LONGVON support smart ring EVT, DVT and PVT?" [level=3]
- paragraph: Yes. For qualified OEM/ODM projects LONGVON discusses an engineering validation path covering EVT (engineering validation), DVT (design validation), PVT (production validation) and transition toward mass production. Stage scope, tooling and fees are confirmed after technical review—this site does not publish fixed price lists.
- heading "Can LONGVON review customer BOM, STEP and Gerber files under NDA?" [level=3]
- paragraph: Yes. Brands that already have industrial design and PCB packages can request a confidential DFM (design-for-manufacturing) review. LONGVON evaluates manufacturability, assembly risk and NPI readiness under an appropriate NDA before quoting development or production work.
- heading "Does LONGVON support custom titanium smart ring enclosures?" [level=3]
- paragraph: Custom enclosure materials—including premium titanium targets—are discussed per project together with sensor windows, charging, sealing and cosmetic requirements. Material choice is confirmed during engineering intake, not as a catalog SKU claim.
- heading "How do sample evaluation and volume pricing work?" [level=3]
- paragraph: LONGVON supports sample / evaluation builds and discusses unit economics across pilot and production quantity bands (for example hundreds to thousands of units). MOQ, tooling and unit pricing are project-specific and provided after RFQ qualification—not as public list prices.
