Frequently Asked Questions

Where is LONGVON based, and who is the manufacturer?

LONGVON Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. is the smart ring manufacturer and OEM/ODM technology provider in Shenzhen, China. MATEYOU is its product brand.

Which MOQ applies to standard OEM and custom ODM?

For standard OEM orders using the existing Ring1 or Ring1C platform, the minimum order quantity (MOQ) is 1,000 units. Product configuration, pricing and other commercial terms are confirmed for each project. Custom ODM projects have separately evaluated quantities and terms. Pilot feasibility is discussed case by case; inquiry-form quantity ranges reflect buyer estimates, not manufacturing offers.

Can we scope hardware, firmware, algorithms, App and data integration together?

Yes. Discuss the required hardware, firmware, health algorithms, App, partner SDK/API and manufacturing responsibilities. Deliverables, permissions and commercial terms are agreed per project; there is no default unrestricted BLE or all-offline algorithm entitlement.
