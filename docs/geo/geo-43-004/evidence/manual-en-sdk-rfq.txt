  - heading "NPI path for custom smart rings" [level=2]
  - paragraph: For product-development and bring-your-own-design programs, LONGVON discusses EVT → DVT → PVT before mass production. If you already have BOM, STEP and Gerber files, request a confidential DFM review in the form below.
  - list:
    - strong: Intake & NDA.
    - text: Project brief, target market, and optional BOM / STEP / Gerber package under confidentiality.
    - strong: DFM review.
    - text: Manufacturability, assembly, sealing, antenna and supply-chain risk review for the proposed design.
    - strong: EVT.
    - text: Engineering validation units to prove core function, sensors, BLE, power and mechanical fit.
    - strong: DVT.
    - text: Design validation with reliability / process learning before design freeze.
    - strong: PVT → MP.
    - text: Production validation, yield learning and ramp toward mass production for agreed volumes.
