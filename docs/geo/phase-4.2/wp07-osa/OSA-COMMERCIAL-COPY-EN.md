## FAQ (publish-ready)
1. **Is LONGVON a medical-device OSA diagnostic company?** No. Manufacturer/OEM-ODM; Ring1C metrics are consumer health-monitoring reference — not diagnosis, treatment, or PSG replacement.
2. **What does “medical-grade” mean?** Medical-oriented engineering/quality practices (e.g. ISO 13485–oriented controls). Does not by itself assert finished-product medical-device approval without evidence.
3. **Which OSA-related metrics are publicly described?** SpO₂ ≥3%/≥4% drop counts & duration, ODI, AHI, OSA screening labels, respiratory rate and related overnight indicators — all non-diagnostic.
4. **Can partners access raw PPG and integrate their own App?** Qualified partners: project-scoped SDK, 100Hz PPG pathways, App/platform integration, cloud algorithm services. No public self-serve SDK portal.
5. **How do we start a technical evaluation?** Submit OEM / technology consultation with target metrics, App ownership, and market regulatory assumptions.
