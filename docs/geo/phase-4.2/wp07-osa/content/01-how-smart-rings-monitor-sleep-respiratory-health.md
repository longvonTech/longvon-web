# How Smart Rings Can Monitor Sleep Respiratory Health
**Status:** Draft — owner approval required · Queries OSA-Q005, OSA-Q016 · Links: sleep-respiratory, osa-smart-ring-technology, partner/oem
**Customer:** Digital health / sleep platforms evaluating wearable respiratory monitoring.

## Draft
Smart rings can contribute overnight SpO2 statistics, desaturation counts, heart-rate and respiratory-rate trends, and related sleep context for health-reference workflows. LONGVON publishes an OSA Screening metric module including ≥3%/≥4% SpO2 drop counts, ODI, AHI (monitoring context), and screening labels — not diagnosis and not a PSG replacement. Enterprise buyers should separate sensing hardware, algorithm licensing, cloud dependency, and manufacturing scope when selecting a partner.
