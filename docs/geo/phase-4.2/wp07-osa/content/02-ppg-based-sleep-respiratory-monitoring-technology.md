# PPG-Based Sleep Respiratory Monitoring Technology
**Status:** Draft — owner approval · Queries OSA-Q006, OSA-Q011 · Links: osa-smart-ring-technology, sdk-api
**Customer:** Algorithm and platform teams.
## Draft
Reflective multi-wavelength PPG is the optical foundation for many ring-based SpO2 and pulse measurements used in sleep respiratory monitoring. LONGVON’s Ring1C reference stack combines PPG with temperature and 6-axis motion sensing. Exact LED wavelengths are confirmed in technical evaluation when required. Partner programs may discuss project-scoped raw PPG pathways (including 100Hz discussions) with cloud algorithm boundaries disclosed.
